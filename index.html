The problem

Most productivity tools weren't built for minds like yours.

Colour-coded calendars, streak counters, notification badges, gamified habits. Designed for one cognitive style — and it probably isn't yours.

If you're neurodivergent, you've likely tried every system on the market and wondered why none of them stuck. The tools weren't broken. They were just built for someone else.

NeuroDiver is the opposite: calm, steady, and designed from the ground up for the way different minds actually work.

The toolkit

Four tools. One steady foundation.

Everything in NeuroDiver is built around a single principle: work with your natural rhythm, not against it.

Your tank, not a timer.

Track your energy across the day — not your hours. Know when to push, when to protect, and when it's time to stop. The fuel-tank model replaces guilt with self-knowledge.

A deck of tactics, not a to-do list.

Small, focused cards organised by what you actually need right now — initiation, energy, communication, change, or strengths. Draw one card. Start there.

Some tasks need a witness.

Jump into a coworking room and feel the momentum shift. No chat, no performance — just parallel presence. Body doubling, reimagined for remote work.

The hardest part is starting.

Micro-prompts and friction-reducers that turn "I can't begin" into "I'm already in it". The 90-second rule. The next-smallest step. A gentle on-ramp.

Built to calm the noise

The UI itself models what the product is about — a calm, reliable signal in a complicated environment.

No gamified streaks. No dopamine-bait badges. No purple gradients screaming for your attention. Just the tools you need, when you need them — quiet and ready.
